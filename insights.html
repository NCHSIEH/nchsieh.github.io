本文整理本人自主研發的長照服務 AI Agent 流程與顧問方法規劃，屬於個人 IP。以下以示意情境說明設計與專業判斷；目前尚未與廠商正式合作或完成商業交付。

This article presents my independent long-term care AI Agent workflow and advisory methodology, developed as personal intellectual property. The scenario is illustrative; these projects have not yet involved formal vendor collaboration or commercial delivery.

自主研發：把單一工具放回完整服務流程Independent R&D: place each tool within the full service workflow

我的長照服務 AI Agent 規劃涵蓋七個階段，從進案資料到需求改變後的重新評估，形成可追蹤的服務循環。每一階段分別標示 AI 輔助、規則檢核、核心系統及人員決策，避免只列出工具功能，卻沒有說明交接責任。

My long-term care AI Agent design spans seven stages, from intake to reassessment when needs change, forming a traceable service cycle. Each stage distinguishes AI support, rule checks, core systems and human decisions so handover responsibilities are explicit.

  1. 進案與資料蒐集Intake & data collection
  2. 需求初篩與正式評估Screening & formal assessment
  3. 照顧計畫形成Care plan formation
  4. 資源媒合與排班Resource matching & scheduling
  5. 服務執行與紀錄Service delivery & records
  6. 持續監測、品質與核銷Monitoring, quality & reconciliation
  7. 需求改變與再評估Changed needs & reassessment

這份規劃展現的核心能力，是把照護情境轉為流程、資料、規則、責任與驗收要求。下面以「臨時缺勤與改派」說明如何將整體架構落到具體工作。

The core capability demonstrated is translating care scenarios into workflows, data, rules, responsibilities and acceptance requirements. The absence and reassignment example below shows how the architecture becomes concrete work.

一個值得先處理的情境A useful starting scenario

照服員臨時缺勤，督導在通訊軟體中反覆找人、確認資格與時段，再通知家屬。原任務是否保留、誰決定替代人選、通知是否完成，若沒有一致紀錄，後續就難以追蹤服務中斷原因，也難以判斷新工具是否有效。

A care worker is unexpectedly absent. A supervisor searches messaging threads for a replacement, checks qualifications and availability, then contacts the family. Without consistent records of the original task, assignment decision and notification, it is difficult to trace service interruptions or evaluate a new tool.

我的判斷：先建立可追蹤的服務流程，才有條件評估 AI 能改善哪一段。

My judgment: establish a traceable service workflow before assessing where AI can help.

第一步是把現場問題整理成具體事件、責任與資料需求。若問題來自規則不明或資料缺漏，先修正流程往往比直接採購完整系統更容易驗證。

Begin by defining events, responsibilities and data needs. When rules are unclear or records incomplete, a focused workflow change may be easier to validate than purchasing a complete system.

把改派拆成三個可驗收的工作Three work packages with clear acceptance criteria

  1. 保留事件與責任Preserve the event and accountability

    保留原任務與異動原因，記錄接手者、決策時間及處理狀態。驗收時，應能重建「何時缺勤、誰接手、如何處理」。

    Keep the original task, change reason, responsible person, decision time and status. Acceptance requires reconstructing when the absence occurred, who took ownership and what happened next.

  2. 先訂排序條件，再推薦候選人Define ranking criteria before recommending candidates

    先篩選必要資格與可服務時段，再比較距離、工時、服務連續性、個案偏好及風險。每次推薦應能說明理由；重要例外由具名人員確認。

    Check required qualifications and availability first, then compare distance, workload, continuity, client preferences and risk. Recommendations should be explainable, with important exceptions confirmed by a named staff member.

  3. 完成通知，才關閉事件Close the event after notification is completed

    區分取消、延遲與替代服務，指定通知對象、期限及逾期處理方式。驗收應看實際通知紀錄與事件關閉情形。

    Distinguish cancellation, delay and replacement. Specify recipients, deadlines and escalation rules. Verify notification records and how events were closed.

AI 與人員的分工要寫清楚Make human and AI responsibilities explicit

可評估由 AI 協助Potential AI support

整理紀錄、產生待辦與候選方案、草擬通知、提醒未結事件。能否採用，仍須檢查資料品質、存取權限與實際試辦表現。

Organize records, produce task lists and candidate options, draft notifications and flag open events. Suitability depends on data quality, access controls and pilot performance.

由具名人員負責Named staff remain accountable

確認候選人適任性、重大例外處理、正式照護計畫核定與最終服務安排。人員需能覆核、修正建議，必要時回到原有流程。

Confirm suitability, handle major exceptions, approve formal care plans and make final service arrangements. Staff need a way to review and revise suggestions, and return to the existing workflow when necessary.

如何判斷試辦值得擴大？How should a pilot earn expansion?

先在範圍明確的服務區建立基線，再用相同定義比較試辦前後。至少同時觀察效率、服務品質與人員負荷，避免將工作轉移給前線卻誤認為改善。

Establish a baseline in a defined service area and compare results using consistent definitions. Examine efficiency, service quality and staff workload together, so work shifted to frontline staff is not mistaken for improvement.

效率Efficiency
改派處理時間、逾期事件、重複處理次數。Reassignment time, overdue events and repeated handling.
服務品質Service quality
未完成服務、通知完成情形、固定服務者比例。Uncompleted visits, notification completion and continuity of care workers.
人員負荷Staff workload
照服員加班、督導補登紀錄時間、前線使用回饋。Care worker overtime, supervisor documentation time and frontline feedback.

前後差異還要檢查服務量、人員配置、同期制度變更及資料缺漏。證據不足時，先補足資料或調整試辦；成效、使用情形與責任機制均有支持時，再討論擴大。不能把示意數字當成成果承諾。

Also examine service volume, staffing, concurrent changes and missing data. Fill evidence gaps or adjust the pilot first; discuss expansion once results, adoption and accountability support it. Illustrative figures are not outcome commitments.

顧問合作可以交付什麼？What can an advisory engagement deliver?

  • 現況流程圖、責任分工與主要斷點清單。Current workflow, responsibility map and key gaps.
  • 資料需求、品質問題與候選方案比較。Data requirements, quality issues and comparison of options.
  • AI 概念驗證或流程改善試辦計畫，含人工覆核與回退方式。AI proof-of-concept or workflow pilot plan, including human review and fallback.
  • 驗收指標、前後比較架構、團隊培訓及擴大條件建議。Acceptance criteria, evaluation framework, team training and expansion recommendations.

實際交付範圍依機構問題、可取得資料及委託內容議定。這些方法也可用於技術提案審查與系統需求釐清。

Scope depends on the institution's needs, available data and agreed engagement. These methods also support technical proposal review and system requirements definition.

USR：讓知識落實於照護現場USR: translating knowledge into care practice

秉持大學社會責任（USR）精神，重視知識分享、人才培育與在地合作。科技導入應讓前線人員理解方法、能持續運用，並以照護品質與人的需求作為檢視方向。

Guided by University Social Responsibility (USR), I value knowledge sharing, talent development and local partnerships. Technology adoption should help frontline teams understand and sustain the approach, with care quality and people's needs guiding evaluation.
