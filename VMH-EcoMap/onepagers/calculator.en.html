Is this project actually profitable?
Ask first: which yardstick should be used?

VMH-EcoMap-2 project business model calculator v1.0| The master toolkit answers “whether to do it, who does it, when to do it” — this file answers only one question:whether this particular project is worthwhile.
Core design: do not measure every project against the same NPV standard. First select the “investment category” on the 01 worksheet tab, and the 10 decision summary will automatically apply the corresponding interpretation rules.
The consultant fills in four pages of costs and revenue, the system calculates six pages,and the owner only needs to look at the last page (10 decision summary). One file per sub-project.
Why categorize? Because using the wrong yardstick can reject the investment that most needs to be made
Take infrastructure such as “server rooms,” “networks,” and “data platforms” — this kind ofinfrastructure, compare it to a “CT imaging center” using the same NPV standard, and the conclusion will always be that infrastructure is not worth investing in — becauseits benefits accrue to the other projects built on top of it, while all the costs remain with it alone. This is not because the project is bad — it is because the wrong yardstick was used. The right question is not “how much does it earn,” but“how much more would it cost over the next ten years if we don’t build it ourselves.”。
MIT Sloan (Weill & Aral) empirical evidence:infrastructure is the category with the highest share of enterprise IT spending (46% on average), and it is explicitly identified as the category that is “most difficult to cost-justify in advance.” The same study also shows:★ Top-performing companies allocate 55% of IT spending to infrastructure, while bottom-performing companies allocate only 37%.

Why this answer Because a reimbursement code exists.Imaging exams have a health-insurance rate (Circular 22/2023/TT-BYT) + a self-pay market, so “billed per procedure performed” holds true — one of thefew cases in Vietnam’s healthcare digitalization where the numbers can be worked out on a standalone basis. NPV is positive, MIRR exceeds WACC, PI is 1.30, though the 7.8-year payback period is somewhat long, so contract duration coverage must be confirmed. Because the right question was asked.Treating the server room as a “rent-collecting business” will inevitably produce a negative conclusion. Instead, ask “how much would we pay FPT/Viettel over ten years if we don’t build it ourselves” —self-build 10-year TCO 104,212, lease 118,600, difference +14,388， The build cost pays for itself in 4 years, while supporting 7 projects. Because the revenue side is locked in by the regulatory structure.Vietnam’s medical service pricing still only covers labor and direct costs,not IT； and the first reimbursement case to include IT (313/QĐ-BYT) has a published price that is still5.9% lower. Demand is being forced out by regulation, but a payment source has not yet been established.
So what should be done Add aguaranteed minimum exam volumeto the contract; also negotiate on patient mix, not just volume —self-pay ratiohas a greater impact on profitability than service volume. Also confirm eligibility for the 10% socialized-healthcare tax rate (the 08 conservative scenario has already tested the outcome of reverting to 20%). Keep an eye on three variables that could flip the conclusion:① electricity tariff classification (Circular 60/2025 commercial tier would raise electricity costs by 45–55%; must confirm with EVN); ② whether staff can share on-call duty with in-house IT (3 FTE vs. 8–9 FTE standalone);③ actual lease quotes. Do not approve the full amount at once.Split into three phases with gates: outpatient go-live + case licensing → inpatient testing go-live + negotiation with the 2nd hospital → hospital-wide paperless + contract signed with the 2nd hospital. The one-time implementation cost is approximately 10,530, which can be amortized down by more than 60% for the 2nd and 3rd hospitals.
Negative NPV ≠ this project cannot be done
It only says, “under the current set of conditions, calculated on a standalone basis,it does not pay back.” First confirmwhether the investment category was selected correctly—— For the three categories infrastructure, regulatory-mandated, and replacement & sustaining, a negative NPV is the norm and does not constitute grounds for rejection.
Three pass conditions for enabling-type projects
① ① ΔTCO present value > 0 (self-build is cheaper than leasing);② ② unit service cost is lower than the market outsourced price; ③ number of projects supported ≥ 2— a “platform” that serves only a single project is, in essence, that project’s cost, not a platform.

is not any particular cost item, it isthe discount rate. The same model can produce opposite conclusions when using 10% versus 13%. The 07 worksheet tab has already derived it using CAPM (including Vietnam’s 3.90% country risk premium), butit must still be confirmed in writing with the investor。
01B Category decision wizard
Answer four questions (does money come in from outside / what happens if it isn’t done / how many projects does it support / will operations stall without it) to get a recommended category, along with the rationale, next steps, and a cross-check against the selection in 01. Includes the four points where users most commonly get stuck.
One-click switching between three currencies
Input defaults to NTD. Changing the “display currency” in 01 automatically converts the entire file to USD or VND; the display unit (unit / thousand / million) can also be switched to avoid overly long VND figures.
BOQ-level cost breakdown
02 and 03 use formal engineering-industry line-item names and a “quantity × unit price” structure, distinguishing imported from local, with built-in import duty andnon-deductible input VAT。
Depreciation and Vietnam tax incentives
Depreciation follows the statutory useful life under Circular 45/2013; the tax rate is not hardcoded — 10% for socialized healthcare + 4-year tax holiday, with the holiday start date following the statutory rule of “whichever comes first: first profitable year vs. 4th year of revenue.”
Working capital
Health-insurance and self-pay receivable days are set separately. Vietnam’s health-insurance settlement often involves suspended over-spending claims (in 2025, hospitals in Ho Chi Minh City had nearly VND 2,500 billion suspended), so a generic assumption cannot be applied.
WACC and FCFF
The discount rate is derived using CAPM: Vietnam 10-year government bond 4.54% + β × mature-market ERP 4.23% + Vietnam country risk premium 3.90% (Damodaran 2026). Cash flow uses unlevered FCFF.
MIRR, PI, ROIC, tornado chart
Because the model includes mid-project replacement investment, IRR may have multiple solutions, so MIRR is used as the primary metric. Also included are PI (for ranking multiple cases), ROIC (compared against WACC), and a tornado chart (to identify the most critical variable).

Remember this file in one sentence:First select the correct category (01; if unsure, use the 01B wizard), the consultant fills in three pages of costs and revenue (02–04), the system calculates six pages (05–09), and the owner reviews one page (10).
When to open a new file:When the business judgment for an item in the master toolkit becomes “proceed immediately (Go),” or when preparing to enter the WP-09 investment decision, open a calculator file for that item.
Must be completed before submitting for investment decision:The ten checks at the bottom of the 10 worksheet tab — formal quotes, import duty and input VAT, tax incentive eligibility,discount rate agreed in writing, regulatory pathway, local OPEX, unit price verification, health-insurance receivable days, contract clauses in place for the top-ranked variable in the tornado chart, and independent-unit review.
