Three rules for reading the diagram: ① For any worksheet tab with a blue arrow pointing into it, never type manually — that value is calculated by a formula, and overwriting it means it will never update again. ② A purple dashed line indicates “where this page’s dropdown options come from”; to add another option, go to the bottom of the list on the page where the arrow originates and add a row. ③ A gray dashed line marks places deliberately not automated: cross-file links can break and can also bring external content into the master toolkit, so copy-pasting a specified range is used instead.
Two closed loops worth noting:“03 ↔ 16” — the capabilities in 03 are mirrored into the matrix, and the business opportunity computed by the matrix is fed back into column AF of 03; “15 ↔ 14” — the dictionary supplies the business opportunity dropdown, and which ones are used in 14 is fed back into the “included in 14” column of the dictionary. In both cases, different columns reference each other, so no circular reference is created.
One point that is easy to misunderstand:06 Action roadmap, 07 Overall project scale, and 10 Decision log contain no cross-tab formulas whatsoever — they are manually maintained work areas; changing 03 will not automatically update them, nor will they be overwritten by 03.
