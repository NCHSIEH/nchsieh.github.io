Vietnam Healthcare Intel Station

The two things that sink a pitch: getting regulatory timing wrong, and being asked "what's that based on?" This page turns signed, effective-dated regulations into a countdown clock, lays out what competing vendors have actually built in Vietnam, and tags every market figure with a source and verification date — anything unverifiable is marked "not found," never invented.

When to use it

Scenario 1　Confirming timing before a pitchThe regulatory clock sorts by days remaining; red means within 120 days. "169 days until this takes effect" carries far more weight than "I heard it's coming next year."
Scenario 2　Getting asked about competitorsClick any company name to expand its profile and past/current hospital projects, each sourced. Knowing what a rival has built tells you where to position.
Scenario 3　Needing a citable number for a deckEvery market-data card carries a source and verification date you can drop straight into a footnote. Anything unrefreshed past 120 days flags itself "needs update."

Three steps

  1. Read the regulatory clock——Not-yet-effective items sort first (soonest first); effective ones sink to the bottom. Each entry states which opportunities it touches and what we should do.
  2. Check competitor activity——Click a company name to open its detail panel; the "Full Vendor Directory" below covers 115 companies across 9 categories, organized around data center / UPS / IDC as the core axis.
  3. Maintain the data yourself——Top-right "✎ Edit Data" lets you edit, add, or delete entries directly on the page, then "⬇ Export Data" saves a new data file; "⟳ Load Update" imports an update file from someone else.

What you'll see

Three sections: Regulatory Clock (countdown and response for 10 signed regulations), Competitor Activity (12 detailed cards with 48 sourced project track records), and Market Data Dashboard (16 indicators) — plus the Full Vendor Directory of 115 companies.

Data & limitations: Intelligence has a half-life. Every entry carries a verification date and auto-flags "needs update" past 120 days. The vendor directory currently lists names and positioning only — not yet individually deep-researched.
