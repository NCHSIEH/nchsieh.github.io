Vietnam Gap Map

"Which province first?" is the first decision in entering the Vietnam market. This map arranges 34 provinces as tiles (under the 2025 administrative merger), click one to see its local facts; six national gap cards below cover structural whitespace across the whole market.

When to use it

Scenario 1　Deciding where to focus firstWhere should resources go? Start by seeing which provinces show concrete hospital-construction activity and gaps on the tile grid.
Scenario 2　Briefing an owner on local opportunityBefore talking to a hospital in a given province, pull up its fact card first — knowing their context changes the conversation.
Scenario 3　Spotting nationwide structural gapsSix national gap cards (radiotherapy equipment, dialysis coverage, data centers, the integration layer...) are structural opportunities that don't depend on any one province.

Three steps

  1. Click a province tile——Local facts and sources expand on the right. A gold border marks a medical-tourism pilot city.
  2. Review the national gap cards——Six cards below cover cross-province structural gaps, each fully sourced.
  3. Update the data——Top-right "⟳ Load Update" imports a new map data file; the monthly schedule also re-verifies provincial news automatically.

What you'll see

34 province tiles (arranged geographically, north/central/south), 11 provinces with detailed facts so far, and 6 national gap cards.

Data & limitations: Tiles are a schematic layout, not a precise geographic projection. 11 provinces currently have detailed facts; the rest are pending. Vietnam merged 63 provinces into 34 in 2025 — this map uses the new system.
