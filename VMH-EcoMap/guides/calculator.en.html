Deal Calculator

Use the wrong method on the same deal and you get the wrong answer. This tool builds in the correct financial method for each of six investment categories — NPV for revenue deals, least-cost comparison for regulatory mandates, crossover-year analysis for replacements — so you just pick the category, drag the sliders, and the conclusion appears.

When to use it

Scenario 1　Live modeling in an owner meetingThe director asks "does this CT scanner pay back in 3 years?" Pick "A Revenue-generating → CT Imaging Center", adjust daily volume and self-pay ratio to their real numbers, and NPV plus payback period appear instantly.
Scenario 2　Making the case to replace old equipmentPick "E Replacement & Sustaining" — the tool plots cumulative cost curves for keeping vs. replacing and marks the crossover year. Far more persuasive than just saying "the new one is cheaper."
Scenario 3　Mandatory spend ahead of a regulatory deadlinePick "C Regulatory-mandated" and compare in-house vs. outsourced total cost. This category isn't about ROI — it's about complying at the lowest cost.

Three steps

  1. Pick a category——Six buttons up top: A Revenue-generating / B Enabling / C Regulatory-mandated / D Cost-reduction / E Replacement & Sustaining / F Strategic positioning. Each has its own parameters and charts.
  2. Load a preset case——Each category ships 3-4 presets (CT imaging, IVF, data center, medical wastewater, BMS energy, UPS replacement, HIS data platform...) — start from whichever is closest.
  3. Adjust and watch it update——Hover the ⓘ next to any field for a plain-language explanation. KPIs and charts recalculate live; the sensitivity chart shows the discount rate at which the deal stops making sense.

What you'll see

Depending on category, the results card shows: Net Present Value (NPV), Modified Internal Rate of Return (MIRR), payback period, total cost of ownership delta (ΔTCO), annual savings, life-cycle cost (LCC) and cost crossover year, or option value. Two charts below show cash flow / cost curves and sensitivity to discount rate.

Data & limitations: Preset case numbers are market-rate estimates for demonstration and training — not an actual quote from any hospital. Replace with real pricing and hospital data before a formal proposal. All calculation runs in your browser; nothing you enter is uploaded.
