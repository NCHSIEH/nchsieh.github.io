Tender War-Board

Tender management isn't about watching a calendar — it's about "how many days left" and "is the paperwork ready." This read-only war-board reads your tender-radar Excel and automatically computes closing-date countdowns, go/no-go distribution, and qualification-document readiness. Data stays in Excel; this just makes status visible at a glance.

When to use it

Scenario 1　Quick status scan before a weekly meetingLoad the latest Excel before the meeting — tenders closing within 14 days auto-flag red and sort to the top, no need to scroll row by row.
Scenario 2　Checking if qualification documents are readyDocument readiness is visible at a glance; documents nearing expiry flag red as a reminder to renew.

Three steps

  1. Prepare your tender-radar Excel——Log tenders and document status in VMH-EcoMap-15 Tender Radar & Qualification Files.xlsx first.
  2. Load the war-board——Click "Load File 15" and select that Excel — parsing happens entirely on your computer; the file is never uploaded.
  3. Update weekly as routine——Scan the procurement site → log into Excel → reload the war-board, and status updates.

What you'll see

Four KPIs: tenders being tracked, closing within 14 days, decided-to-bid count, and qualification-document readiness rate; tender cards sorted by closing date; document list sorted by urgency.

Data & limitations: This is a read-only tool. All data maintenance happens in Excel — the war-board itself stores no edits.
