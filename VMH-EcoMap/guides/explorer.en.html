Ecosystem Explorer

Vietnam's hospital market isn't one business — it's 85. This explorer lays them out across a seven-layer framework, from physical infrastructure at the base up to business models and cash flow at the top, so you can see which layer each opportunity sits in, what capabilities it needs, which regulation drives it, and where the trend is heading.

When to use it

Scenario 1　Helping a partner self-positionWhen a partner asks "which piece can I do," the layer framework points to their spot instantly, plus who sits upstream and downstream.
Scenario 2　Projecting in a planning meetingClick through live in the room — far faster than flipping through a spreadsheet, and no one misreads a column.
Scenario 3　Finding whitespaceLook for layers with many opportunities but few matching capabilities — e.g. L5 Intelligent Applications has 14 opportunities and almost no capability coverage. That's a gap.

Three steps

  1. Browse by layer or category——Seven layers: L1 Physical Infrastructure / L2 Digital Infrastructure / L3 Clinical Equipment / L4 Data Platform / L5 Intelligent Applications / L6 Care Operations / L7 Business Model & Cash Flow, plus cross-cutting G Governance & Compliance.
  2. Click a card for detail——Each opportunity shows its description, trend (past/present/future), regulatory drivers, required capabilities, and sources.
  3. (Advanced) Load your own Excel——If you have the master Excel toolkit, load it directly for the latest data — parsing happens entirely on your computer; the file is never uploaded.

What you'll see

Full cards for all 85 opportunities, a 10-capability inventory, a capability×opportunity cross matrix, and a seven-layer distribution view.

Data & limitations: This page ships with a demonstration snapshot. For actual decisions, load your own maintained Excel master toolkit for the latest content.
